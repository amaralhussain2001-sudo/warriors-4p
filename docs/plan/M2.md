# M2 — Four genuine players (exit criteria written before work started, 2026-10-11)

Goal (plan §10 row M2, §5): four genuine players in one story mission, each driven by its own input, processed by the
game's own human/combat/AI systems, with its own camera, view and HUD. Engine extension in the game patch plus the W4P
module (up to 4 slots already supported by the lockstep core). Test environment: SINGLE-PC.

## Exit criteria (all required)
| # | Criterion | Measurement / evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Four players exist:** GameMgr player count 4; four distinct human objects registered as players 1-4 | PINE reads of GameMgr (+0x224 count, player handles), log |
| 2 | **Independent input:** each player moves only from its own input (one player at a time moves while the other three are neutral) | position traces per player, scripted inputs |
| 3 | **Same systems as players 1-2:** players 3 and 4 can attack, grab, take damage, be knocked down and recover; enemies target and hit players 3 and 4 | health/state reads over a combat run; screenshots |
| 4 | **Views and HUD:** four views in the 2x2 layout; each instance shows its own player's quarter full screen with that player's radar and health inside the view | screenshots from 4 instances |
| 5 | **Lockstep with 4 instances:** 4 instances on loopback, bots on all slots, ≥ 30 min of mission gameplay, 0 hash mismatches, no crash or softlock | session logs |
| 6 | **Frame rate:** game frame rate ≥ 29 fps (30 fps mode) during the 4-player run; 60 fps mode measured and reported | probe JSON |
| 7 | **Originals unchanged** | `tools/hash_inputs.py` |

Out of scope for M2: all missions (M6), merge rule for 4 (D8 made views always separate), session policies, internet.
Labels: SINGLE-PC, bots; human checks optional.
